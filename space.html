Mobility App · Marketplace — 2025

Space Saver.

A peer-to-peer parking and mobility app that helps drivers find and book a spot while letting property owners rent out space they aren't using — with cab booking and vehicle insurance folded into the same everyday flow.

RoleUI/UX design, marketplace flows
UsersDrivers searching for parking · owners renting space
OutputEnd-to-end app case study
Problem

Parking is a three-app problem disguised as a one-app errand.

01

Spots go unused

Private driveways and lots sit empty all day while nearby drivers circle the block looking for a space.

02

Fragmented booking

Parking, a cab onward, and insurance for the trip live in three different apps with three different logins.

03

No trust signal

Renting a stranger's driveway — or your own to a stranger — needs a lightweight way to feel safe doing it.

Solution

Book the spot, the ride, and the cover — in one flow.

01

Peer-to-peer listings

Owners list unused parking space in minutes; drivers search it like any other spot.

02

Live availability

Real-time spot status removes the guesswork of driving up to a "maybe."

03

Cab booking

Once parked, a cab for the rest of the trip is one tap away in the same app.

04

Built-in insurance

Optional vehicle cover attaches directly to the booking, not a separate policy hunt.
