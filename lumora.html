Problem

A syllabus moves at one pace. A student never does.

01

One-size pacing

Course content ships at a fixed speed regardless of who's already ahead and who's quietly falling behind.

02

Delayed feedback

By the time a gap in understanding shows up in a grade, the moment to address it directly has usually passed.

03

Generic review

Revision material treats every student's weak spot as the same weak spot.

Concept

A path that adjusts itself, in the open.

01

Adaptive sequencing

The next topic is chosen from how the last one actually went, not a fixed curriculum order.

02

Visible reasoning

The student can see why the path changed — the system explains its own adjustments, in plain language.

03

Targeted review

Revision surfaces the specific concept that tripped someone up, not a generic recap of the whole unit.
