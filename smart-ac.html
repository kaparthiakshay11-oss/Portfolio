A bedside device that
reads your body, not the room.

Smart Sleep‑Based AC Control is an ESP32‑powered system that tracks how thermal comfort shifts through the night and adjusts the air conditioner automatically — so the room never has to guess, and the sleeper never has to wake up to fix it.

Problem

Static cooling fights a body that never stops changing.

01

Too hot, too cold

Sleepers wake up thermally uncomfortable, fragmenting rest and recovery cycles they're not even aware of losing.

02

Fixed AC settings

The unit holds one temperature all night, indifferent to how the body's needs shift between 10pm and dawn.

03

Manual interruption

Reaching for the remote means waking up fully — the fix costs the same sleep it's trying to protect.

Key insight

Comfort is driven by body temperature, not room temperature.

Core body temperature drops at sleep onset and climbs again toward morning — a rhythm every static AC ignores. Scrub through a typical night below to see how the gap between room and body opens and closes.

Even a 1–2°C deviation from the comfort band is enough to pull a sleeper from deep sleep into light sleep. Conventional units run on a timer or a single manual setting — they have no signal for this at all.
