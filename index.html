About

Design, run through a maker's hands.

"I'm a Bachelor of Design student specialising in UI/UX at PES University, with a foundation in product design and visual communication."

I focus on solving real problems through user‑centred design — starting with how people actually behave, not how a flowchart assumes they will. Good design, to me, is simple, intuitive, and purposeful: understand the user, isolate the real problem, then translate that insight into something clear enough to build. I hold usability and visual craft to the same standard — one without the other isn't finished work.
